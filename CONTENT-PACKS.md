# Velvet Grimoire Starter Content Packs

These are ready directions for future demo expansions. Each pack should include 8-12 scenes, one map, 3-5 NPCs, ward examples, and one safe public screenshot path.

## Dark Court Intrigue

Masked bargains, family secrets, patronage, duels, and overheard vows.

Good for demonstrating:
- Authority wards
- Betrayal and manipulation
- Fawn as tactical diplomacy
- Social maps with locked rooms

## Haunted Manor

A house that remembers guests, rearranges doors, and repeats unfinished scenes.

Good for demonstrating:
- Horror and surveillance wards
- Threshold and Hearth importance
- Map events and fog of war
- Quiet Freeze scenes

## Dungeon Bargain

A locked descent where every room offers a price, shortcut, or confession.

Good for demonstrating:
- Captivity as a hard-gated ward
- Traps and loot events
- Fight / Flight pressure
- Veiled content downgrade

## Fey Pact

Promises, names, gifts, etiquette, and dangerous hospitality.

Good for demonstrating:
- Contract scenes
- Banished Names
- Consensual-if framing
- Social risk without explicit content

## City Underworld

Canals, rooftops, guild debts, safe houses, and double-crosses.

Good for demonstrating:
- Pursuit and surveillance
- Scene-linked map travel
- Rival NPCs
- Backup/export after a longer campaign

## Rival Adventurer

One recurring opponent who can become enemy, ally, mirror, or witness.

Good for demonstrating:
- Branch consequences
- Scene templates
- Player-facing response notes
- Session log review
